Dataset notes - Colombo metro delivery routing
================================================
Files
- colombo_delivery_locations.csv : 100 nodes = 1 depot (Colombo Fort) + 99 named localities in the
  Colombo / Gampaha / Kalutara corridor. Columns: node_id, location_name, latitude, longitude, node_type.
- distance_matrix_km.csv         : derived file. Pairwise Haversine great-circle distances (km) computed
  from the locations file by src/build_matrix.py. The solvers recompute distances from the locations
  file; the matrix CSV is kept for transparency and is checked for consistency by src/validate_data.py.
- osm_validation.csv             : cross-check of every locality against OpenStreetMap (Nominatim),
  retrieved 2026-10-14 by src/validate_against_osm.py.

Provenance
The locality names are real places. Coordinates are approximate representative locality-centre points
curated by the team; they are NOT customer addresses. To check them, every name was geocoded against
OpenStreetMap: 95 of 102 original names returned a match, the median offset was 0.75 km and 77 of 95 were
within 2 km. Offsets above 3 km were reviewed by hand (column "status" in osm_validation.csv).

Corrections made after the OSM check (original file had 102 rows)
- Bandaragama: latitude was 6.519 (21.7 km off); corrected to OSM place node 6.7145, 79.989.
- Ganemulla: was 5.2 km from the OSM place node; corrected to 7.0683, 79.9614.
- Kochchikade renamed "Kochchikade (Negombo)" because a Colombo-15 locality has the same name.
- "Pannipitiya Junction" and "Moratuwa Town" removed: they had exactly the same coordinates as
  "Pannipitiya" and "Moratuwa" (zero distance), which adds no routing information.
- node_id values were renumbered 0..99. osm_validation.csv keeps the ORIGINAL ids in "original_node_id".
Several localities remain very close together (e.g. Narahenpita/Thimbirigasyaya 0.11 km); they are
distinct places, but they make the instance slightly easier than a set of well-separated customers.

Known limitations
- Distances are straight-line Haversine distances, which are shorter than road distances.
- One vehicle, no demands, capacities, time windows or traffic.
- Nominatim sometimes returned a homonym or a district centroid; those cases were not used to move points.
