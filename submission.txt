Optimization Methods - Programming Assignment
Problem: Colombo metro delivery route optimisation (single-vehicle Travelling Salesperson Problem, 1 depot + 99 localities).
Dataset: data/colombo_delivery_locations.csv - 100 nodes, real locality names with approximate centre coordinates,
         cross-checked against OpenStreetMap (data/osm_validation.csv). Derived Haversine matrix: data/distance_matrix_km.csv.
Methods: Exact Held-Karp dynamic programming (optimal up to 20 nodes); heuristics Nearest Neighbour, 2-opt,
         2-opt + Or-opt, multi-start 2-opt and iterated local search (100 nodes).
